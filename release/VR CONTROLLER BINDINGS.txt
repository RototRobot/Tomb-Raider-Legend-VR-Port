================================================================
  Tomb Raider: Legend VR - Controller Bindings
================================================================

The VR controllers send the game's normal keyboard and mouse controls.
The keyboard and mouse continue to work at the same time.


HP REVERB G2 / OCULUS TOUCH
----------------------------
Left stick                  Move Lara (W / A / S / D)
Left stick short click      Pause; menu back (Escape)
Left stick double click     Recenter the VR view
Left stick long click       Use health pack (Home)

Right stick                 Turn the camera (mouse movement)
Right stick short click     Switch weapons (End)
Right stick long click      Use binoculars (Page Down)

Left trigger                Action / interact / pull grapple (E)
Right trigger               Fire (left mouse); menu select (Enter)
Left grip                   Throw grapple (Q)
Right grip                  Combat lock-on (G)

Right A                     Jump / surface (Space); menu select (Enter)
Right B                     Dive (F); menu back (Escape)
Left X                      Accurate/free aim (Z)
Left Y short press          Grenade / flare (K)
Left Y long press           Personal light / flashlight (Delete)
Left Y held                 Board mode: flick Lara over (board_god_hand)

MENU CONTROLS
-------------
Left stick                  Navigate (arrow keys)
Jump button                 Select (Enter)
Right trigger               Select (Enter)
Dive button                 Back (Escape)
Pause button                Back (Escape)

The same SteamVR action set remains active in gameplay and menus. This avoids
the separate menu-binding set that some controllers left completely unbound.


OPTIONAL IMMERSIVE MODE
-----------------------
Set this in trlvr.ini and restart the game:

    [controls]
    immersive_controls = 1

FIRST PERSON (also set [vr] first_person = 1, first_person_tracked_hands = 1)
The grips become your hands; changes from the tables above:
  * Pistols: grip at a hip to draw (akimbo draws both); grip there again to
    holster. Grip over a shoulder to draw or swap to the long gun; the other
    hand on its foregrip holds it two-handed.
  * Belt: grab the grapple (fling and let go to throw) or the binoculars
    (bring them to your face). Pouches: grenade/flare at the front of the belt
    (fling to throw), medipack on the right chest (bring it to your mouth).
  * Personal light: left grip at the left chest.
  * Action trigger: precision aim with guns out, otherwise Action (E). With the
    grapple attached, hold a trigger and pull your hand back to yank.
  * Climbing: grip ledges, vines, chains and bars; pull to move, pull down
    with both hands to climb up.
  * X, Y long press and right stick long press are not used (the gestures
    above replace them). The right stick turns smoothly or in snaps
    (turn_mode). left_handed = 1 swaps the triggers' roles.

THIRD PERSON
The full button layout above still works. In addition a floating gear cross
of the game's own item icons sits in front of you: grip an icon to use it,
grip its centre handle to move the cross. The left grip at the left chest
toggles the light.

In first person, pause menu > VR Holster Setup places the holsters, belt
gear and pouches for your body: hold each item where you want it and pull the
trigger.


NOTES
-----
* A long press is approximately 0.65 seconds.
* F1 on the keyboard recentres the view. Double-clicking the left stick does
  the same from every supplied VR controller binding.
* If the long-press actions do not appear, SteamVR may have retained an older
  or custom binding. Open SteamVR > Settings > Controllers > Manage Controller
  Bindings and select/reset to the supplied Tomb Raider: Legend VR binding.
* Tutorial and contextual prompts translate the game's coloured keyboard token
  to the matching VR control name. Normal prose is left untouched.
